How benchmarks work

A published average describes that study’s players. It is not a qualifying score, a percentile or a prediction of selection. Match the population, method, surface and trial rule before comparing. Independent studies do not create a precise club-to-international ladder.

Mean is the group average. Standard deviation describes spread around that mean; it is not a published minimum–maximum range. A mean ± SD does not supply a pass band or percentile.

Available references include club, collegiate, elite domestic, professional and international cohorts. They cover different tests, methods and populations. Numerical references live on the relevant assessment pages.
