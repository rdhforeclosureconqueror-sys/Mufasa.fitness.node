TITLE: GYMNASTICS FUNDAMENTALS BASE – LEVEL 3–5

GOAL:
Define foundational qualities an Olympic-style coach should build from Level 3–5 that support later progression to Level 8–Elite for Marleigh.

GLOBAL PRIORITIES
- BODY SHAPE CONTROL
  Hollow, arch, straight, and tight transitions between them.
- LINE AWARENESS
  Handstand line, toe point, knee extension, hip extension.
- LANDING MECHANICS
  Soft, quiet, knees tracking over toes, absorbing force through hips.
- MENTAL REPETITION
  Visualization and consistent pre-routine checklist.

KEY FUNDAMENTAL SKILLS BY CATEGORY (SUMMARY, NOT OFFICIAL ROUTINES)

1) FLOOR
- Forward and backward rolls into tight shapes.
- Handstand hold against wall → free-standing progressions.
- Cartwheel, round-off mechanics (straight arms, blocked shoulders).
- Back and front walkover progressions (bridge, kickover, limber).
- Jumps: straight jump, tuck, straddle, split, rebound control.

2) BEAM
- Walks: forward, backward, sideways with posture and toe point.
- Static holds: arabesque, passe, lunge + high relevé.
- Levers and T-shapes for balance and body line.
- Basic acro entries: handstand step-down drills on line then low beam.
- Dismount basics: small jumps, straight and tuck off beam with sticks.

3) BARS
- Swing basics: tight hollow/arch in long hang and glide swings.
- Beat swings, tap swings, casting to horizontal.
- Pullover and back hip circle progressions.
- Strict hollow and active shoulder shaping in support and hang.

4) VAULT
- Sprint mechanics: fast, relaxed arm drive.
- Board work: straight rebound jumps, stick control.
- Handstand flatback basics: powerful, straight-line block, tight landing.

PHYSICAL PREP TO SUPPORT THESE LEVELS
- Core: hollow rocks, arch rocks, V-ups, leg raises.
- Shoulder strength: wall handstand shrugs, pike push-ups, band Y/T.
- Hip strength/flexibility: hip bridges, lunges, active splits, hip circles.
- Ankle/Achilles resilience: pogo hops, calf raises, balance drills.

COACHING PRINCIPLES
- Every practice: include shapes (hollow/arch), lines (handstand/arabesque), and landings.
- Early focus on soft, consistent landings protects knees and spine as difficulty rises.
- Quality > difficulty: do not level up a skill until shapes and landings are repeatable.
