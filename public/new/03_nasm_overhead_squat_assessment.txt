TITLE: OVERHEAD SQUAT ASSESSMENT – CHECKLIST AND CORRECTIONS

PURPOSE:
Quick whole-body screen. Arms overhead, feet hip-width, athlete squats to comfortable depth while the coach views from:
- FRONT
- SIDE
- BACK (optional)

CUES:
- Feet hip-width, straight ahead.
- Arms straight overhead, biceps by ears.
- Brace core gently, chest tall.
- Squat as deep as comfortable without pain.

FRONT VIEW COMPENSATIONS:
1) FEET TURN OUT
   Possible contributors: tight calves (lateral gastrocnemius, soleus), tight lateral hip rotators; weak medial hamstrings and glute medius.
   Fix strategies:
   - SMR: calves, lateral hip.
   - Stretch: calves, hip external rotators.
   - Strengthen: glute med side steps, single-leg balance, medial ham curls.

2) KNEES MOVE INWARD (VALGUS)
   Possible contributors: tight adductors, TFL; weak glute med/max.
   Fix strategies:
   - SMR: IT band/TFL, adductors.
   - Stretch: hip flexors, adductors.
   - Strengthen: glute bridges, monster walks, mini-band squats with knees out cue.

SIDE VIEW COMPENSATIONS:
3) EXCESSIVE FORWARD LEAN
   Possible contributors: tight hip flexors, calves; weak glutes, core.
   Fix:
   - SMR: calves, quads, hip flexors.
   - Stretch: hip flexors, quads.
   - Strengthen: glute bridge / hip thrust, bird dog, anti-extension core work.

4) LOW BACK ARCHES EXCESSIVELY
   Possible contributors: tight hip flexors and lumbar extensors; weak glute max and deep abs.
   Fix:
   - SMR: hip flexors, lumbar erectors (gentle).
   - Stretch: hip flexors.
   - Strengthen: dead bug, plank, glute bridges, posterior pelvic tilt training.

5) ARMS FALL FORWARD
   Possible contributors: tight lats, pecs; weak mid/lower traps and rotator cuff.
   Fix:
   - SMR: lats, pecs.
   - Stretch: lats, pecs.
   - Strengthen: wall slides, prone Y and T raises, band external rotations.

HOW TO USE:
- For each compensation, log which side and severity (mild, moderate, severe).
- For Marleigh’s gymnastics, link each compensation to event-specific risk:
  - Knees in → higher ACL risk on landings.
  - Arms forward → instability in handstands and tumbling.
- Create prehab blocks that target her specific patterns before full training.
