The eight limbs

  1. Yama — how we relate to others
  2. Niyama — personal observances
  3. Asana — posture and steady embodiment
  4. Pranayama — conscious breath regulation
  5. Pratyahara — turning attention inward
  6. Dharana — focused concentration
  7. Dhyana — meditation
  8. Samadhi — integration and absorption
