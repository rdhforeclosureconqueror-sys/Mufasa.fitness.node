What are the Eight Limbs?

The Eight Limbs are a yoga framework: Yama (how we relate), Niyama (personal practices), Asana (posture), Pranayama (breath practice), Pratyahara (turning inward), Dharana (focused attention), Dhyana (meditation), and Samadhi (integration or connection).

What are Adinkra symbols?

Adinkra are visual symbols associated with Akan peoples, especially in present-day Ghana and Côte d’Ivoire. Their meanings carry through language, art, cloth and community practice. We name the specific program meaning we are working with rather than flattening a symbol into a decoration.
