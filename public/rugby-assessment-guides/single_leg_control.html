Single-leg control and landing

Control of a defined single-leg squat or landing, observed on each side.

Why it matters in rugby

Running and direction changes repeatedly require one leg to support and manage the body.

What this cannot tell you

Task height, support, fatigue and depth change the challenge. A balance task does not establish tackling ability.

How to perform and record it

Coach fixes squat depth/support and an appropriate landing task separately. Record left/right control, trunk movement, balance and symptoms. Start with supported or low-demand tasks when appropriate.
