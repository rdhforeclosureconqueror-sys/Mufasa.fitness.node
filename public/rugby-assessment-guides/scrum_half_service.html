Scrum-half service and tempo

Delivery accuracy, footwork and tempo from a ground ball, both ways.

Why it matters in rugby

Usable service gives the next player time and space to execute.

What this cannot tell you

Pass accuracy and choosing the correct option are different. Fix ball presentation, distance and timing before comparing.

How to perform and record it

Use fixed pass targets and distances from a ground ball both ways. Record accuracy, delivery time if measurable, footwork and communication. Add changing distribute/carry/kick cues only after baseline service.
