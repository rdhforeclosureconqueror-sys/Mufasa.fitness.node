Attack and support decisions

Scanning, support depth, timing and choice in a defined attacking picture.

Why it matters in rugby

A player contributes by creating and using space, including when they do not receive the ball.

What this cannot tell you

A try does not prove the choice was correct. Simulator decisions and live field execution are different evidence.

How to perform and record it

Fix 2v1 or 3v2 starting geometry and vary a documented defender response. Observe scan, depth, timing, support line and pass/carry/kick decision.
