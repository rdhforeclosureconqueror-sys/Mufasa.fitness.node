Role-specific scrum competence

Demonstrated competence in the assigned role and stage of the existing scrum progression.

Why it matters in rugby

Scrum performance is coordinated unit work requiring appropriate individual preparation.

What this cannot tell you

Strength results cannot grant front-row eligibility. Current competition requirements and qualified assessment remain separate.

How to perform and record it

Reference the existing Scrum Ready progression and current qualified coach review. Record exact role, stage demonstrated, body/bind control and unit context. Preserve separate front-row participation/competition authorization.
