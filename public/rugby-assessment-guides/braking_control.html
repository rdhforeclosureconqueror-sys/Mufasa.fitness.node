Sprint-to-stop observation

How the player controls an approach and stops inside a defined zone.

Why it matters in rugby

A player must arrive balanced to change direction or organise for a tackle.

What this cannot tell you

Approach speed changes braking demands. A short stop from jogging cannot be compared with a full-speed stop.

How to perform and record it

Mark a fixed approach lane and stopping zone. Coach selects safe approach speed, fixes the approach distance and zone, and films front/side views. Record steps, trunk control, balance and zone completion; test both lead-leg patterns.
