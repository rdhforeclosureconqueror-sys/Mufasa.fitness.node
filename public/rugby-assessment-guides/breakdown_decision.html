Ruck arrival and decision

Arrival, legal positioning and the choice to compete, support or reload.

Why it matters in rugby

Effective contribution requires reaching the right place and making a useful legal decision.

What this cannot tell you

Being first to the breakdown is not automatically a successful contribution. Current laws and contact supervision apply.

How to perform and record it

Use coached scenarios to observe arrival angle, legal entry, body control and compete/support/reload decision. Start with a walkthrough and record the permitted contact level.
