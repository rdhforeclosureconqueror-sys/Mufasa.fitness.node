Aerial skill and backfield decisions

Positioning, communication, catch security, landing and the next decision around a kick.

Why it matters in rugby

Securing and using kicked possession links backfield coverage to counterattack or territory.

What this cannot tell you

Uncontested catching and a pressured aerial contest are different tasks. Opposed work must follow appropriate coaching progressions.

How to perform and record it

Fix kick type, distance, chase pressure and space. Start with uncontested catches; record positioning, communication, catch security, landing and next decision.
