Standing broad jump

Horizontal distance from a standing two-foot take-off to the nearest heel on landing.

Why it matters in rugby

Adds a horizontal explosive task that complements vertical jump and sprint observations.

What this cannot tell you

It is not a direct measure of acceleration, and a long jump does not prove contact skill.

How to perform and record it

Start with toes behind the line, two-foot take-off, stable two-foot landing; measure to the nearest heel. Fix arm-swing policy, trials and rest.

Setup and valid-result checklist
  • No fall or extra step; record take-off/landing surface; this is an alternative measure, not convertible to CMJ.

What is a good score?

A useful score is a valid measurement that helps answer your development question. Compare your own baseline under the same procedure, then use matched research cohorts as context.

A published average describes that study’s players. It is not a qualifying score, a percentile or a prediction of selection. Match the population, method, surface and trial rule before comparing. Independent studies do not create a precise club-to-international ladder.
