strength · Measured output

Familiar lower-body strength test

Load completed in a specific familiar lower-body lift and repetition scheme.

Why it matters in rugby

Force capacity can support running, lifting and contact when technique allows the player to use it.

What this cannot tell you

Front squat, back squat, box squat, deadlift, 1RM and submaximal tests are separate protocols. Absolute load and load relative to body mass answer different questions.

How to perform and record it

Coach selects one familiar lift and fixed repetition target, technique/range, equipment and supervision. Save each lift/repetition variant as its own definition. Record completed reps and body mass separately; use a technique-limited submaximal test when maximum testing is inappropriate.

Setup and valid-result checklist
  • Same lift, equipment, repetitions and range for retests; stop before loss of technique; estimated and actual 1RM remain distinct.

What is a good score?

A useful score is a valid measurement that helps answer your development question. Compare your own baseline under the same procedure, then use matched research cohorts as context.

A published average describes that study’s players. It is not a qualifying score, a percentile or a prediction of selection. Match the population, method, surface and trial rule before comparing. Independent studies do not create a precise club-to-international ladder.
