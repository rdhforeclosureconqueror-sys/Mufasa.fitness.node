power · Measured output

Countermovement jump

Height reached in a countermovement jump using the chosen measurement method.

Why it matters in rugby

Provides an indication of explosive lower-body output to inform development and track change.

What this cannot tell you

Height is not the same as peak power in watts. Device method, arm use and landing technique change the result.

How to perform and record it

Use a consistent validated device and its method. Fix hands-on-hips condition, take-off/landing instructions and recovery; retain valid trials. Do not compare flight-time and jump-and-reach methods as equivalent.

Setup and valid-result checklist
  • Hands remain in the specified position; stable landing; device and calculation method recorded.

What is a good score?

A useful score is a valid measurement that helps answer your development question. Compare your own baseline under the same procedure, then use matched research cohorts as context.

A published average describes that study’s players. It is not a qualifying score, a percentile or a prediction of selection. Match the population, method, surface and trial rule before comparing. Independent studies do not create a precise club-to-international ladder.
