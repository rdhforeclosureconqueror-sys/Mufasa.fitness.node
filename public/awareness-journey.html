The central practice

Noticing is the beginning. Returning is the practice.

Your mind may wander during meditation. That is normal. When you notice, gently return to your breath, a sound, or another chosen anchor. You do not need to empty your mind.

What is meditation?

Meditation includes practices of attention and awareness. Some use a single anchor; others observe experience more openly. The goal is not to force thoughts to disappear.

What is pranayama?

Pranayama refers to intentional breathing practices in yoga. In traditional teaching, prana is often described as vital energy; this is a spiritual concept, not another scientific term for oxygen. Beginners can start with comfortable, unforced breathing.

Why can breathing feel calming?

The sympathetic nervous system helps mobilize us for demands; the parasympathetic system supports recovery. Both are useful. Gentle, slower breathing can support calm for some people, but it is not an instant switch or a medical treatment.
