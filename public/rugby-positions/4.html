Position 4 · forwards

Lock 4

Connect the scrum unit, contribute to lineout possession as an assigned jumper or lifter, and arrive effectively at tackles, carries and breakdowns.

How we develop this role

Develop strength, movement, jump/lift coordination and repeated involvement. Record the actual lineout role; jersey 4 does not automatically mean jumper or caller.

  • Scrum connection
  • Assigned jump/lift timing and secure delivery
  • Repeat collision work and field arrival

Assign jumper/lifter and caller duties explicitly; jersey number does not define lineout role.

What counts as a good result?

Start with valid technique and the same procedure each time. Review your trend, role demands and field execution with your coach. Each test guide shows published references when a usable source is available.

A published average describes that study’s players. It is not a qualifying score, a percentile or a prediction of selection. Match the population, method, surface and trial rule before comparing. Independent studies do not create a precise club-to-international ladder.
