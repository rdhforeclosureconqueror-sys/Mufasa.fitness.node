    Match-week decision guide

    Recently played?

    Prioritize sleep, food, hydration and comfortable movement. Skip extra high-intensity work when fatigued.

    Feeling recovered?

    Keep speed exposure brief and high quality only when it fits your team's workload and you're ready.

    In a longer break?

    Consider progressive training with your coaches; a bye week alone doesn't guarantee recovery.

    Pain, concussion symptoms or other injury concerns: stop activity and seek appropriate professional evaluation. This program does not provide diagnosis, clearance or return-to-play decisions.
