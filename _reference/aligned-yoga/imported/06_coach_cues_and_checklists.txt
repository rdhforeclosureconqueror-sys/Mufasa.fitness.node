TITLE: UNIVERSAL COACHING CUES AND CHECKLISTS

UNIVERSAL CUES (SAFE AND EFFECTIVE)
- “Tall chest, long spine.”
- “Squeeze glutes, zip up your core.”
- “Knees track over toes.”
- “Land soft and quiet.”
- “Reach long, then snap tight.”
- “Eyes on your focus point.”

BREATHING
- Strength moves: inhale on easier phase, exhale on effort.
- Static holds: slow nasal inhale, controlled exhale, avoid breath holding except for very short maximal efforts.
- Tumbling: emphasize relaxed breathing between passes and at setup.

SESSION CHECKLIST FOR YOUNG ATHLETE (LIKE MARLEIGH)
1) Warm-up done? (sweat lightly, heart rate up)
2) Shapes practiced? (hollow, arch, straight)
3) Landings rehearsed? (sticks from small height)
4) Strength block completed? (3–4 key patterns)
5) Event goals touched? (at least one focus per event)
6) Cooling down / stretching and reflection logged?

SAFETY NOTES
- Pain in spine, knees, or wrists that persists = stop and evaluate.
- Quality of movement always beats number of reps or difficulty.
- Fatigue reduces skill quality; do big skills early in the session.

MINDSET CUES
- “Strong and smooth, not rushed.”
- “One rep at a time.”
- “We train to own the skill, not just survive it.”
