# Notification launch runbook

Enable the complete gamification pipeline and `GAMIFICATION_NOTIFICATIONS=true`, verify persistent storage, then use Launch Health to confirm service, projection, routes, UI, writability, and deduplication. Acceptance: complete a fixture workout, observe one unread item, replay events, confirm it remains one, read and dismiss it as the owner, and confirm another member cannot address it. Roll back by disabling the flag; retain the notification file for audit and re-enable/reproject later.
