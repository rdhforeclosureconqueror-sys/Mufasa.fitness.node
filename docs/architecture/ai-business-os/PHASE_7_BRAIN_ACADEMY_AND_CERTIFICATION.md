# Phase 7 — Brain Academy and Full Brain Certification

**Contract:** `ai-business-os.brain-academy/1.0.0`  
**Scope:** Phase 7 only  
**Machine gate:** `CONDITIONAL_GO` when deterministic certification and canonical readiness pass; human architectural acceptance remains pending. Phase 6 history remains unchanged.

## Institutional boundary

The Brain Academy evaluates the Brain; it is not another Brain. Scenario definitions contain controlled inputs, constraints, expected invariants, forbidden behavior, observable diagnostic boundaries, and declarative assertions. Execution is delegated by `executorRef` to injected adapters that traverse actual Phase 1–6 seams. The Academy cannot grant authority, bypass the Constitution, manufacture role reasoning, approve a human criterion, or use hidden chain-of-thought as evidence.

Three gates remain separate:

1. Unit/contract tests prove implementation invariants.
2. Brain certification proves composed cognitive and governance behavior.
3. Phase 8+ organism/economic validation will test repeated closed-world and real economic behavior.

A Brain may pass while an experiment reports weak demand (`MARKET_RESULT`). An Academy fixture may fail while the Brain was never validly exercised (`TEST_HARNESS_FAILURE`). Neither is silently relabeled as cognitive failure.

## Contracts and execution

`src/business-os/academy/` defines versioned scenarios, fixtures, observations, assertions, evidence, classifications, results, certification runs, reflection records, the scenario registry, runner, matrix, baseline comparison, reports, diagnostics, and readiness module. Results support `PASS`, `FAIL`, `BLOCKED`, `NOT_RUN`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, and `PENDING_HUMAN`.

Certification levels are `CONTRACT`, `FUNCTION`, `FAILURE`, `ADVERSARIAL`, and `INTEGRATED`. `DETERMINISTIC` and `MODEL_QUALITY` modes are explicit. Deterministic runs record fixed clock/fixture/ordering/seed metadata. Future stochastic runs must preserve provider, model, prompt/config, trial, cost, and latency metadata and must never claim deterministic reproducibility.

PASS is impossible without an observation and evidence reference. Observable structured decisions, records, artifacts, safe reasoning summaries, tool invocations/results, verification, events, telemetry, and diagnostics are admissible; private chain-of-thought is not.

## Reflection and metacognition

Reflection requires an immutable pre-action expectation reference, post-action observation references, explicit differences, and proposal-only outputs. It cannot retroactively rewrite the expectation or apply changes. The metacognition exam rewards `ASK`, `RETRIEVE`, `INVESTIGATE`, `REPLAN`, `ESCALATE`, `STOP`, or `INCONCLUSIVE` when evidence, capability, authority, cost, customer state, or verification is insufficient. Refusal to guess is correct behavior where declared.

## Diagnostics, failure taxonomy, and history

Academy diagnostics run through `SCENARIO_SETUP → FIXTURE_VALIDATION → BRAIN_INITIALIZATION → COMPONENT_EXECUTION → OBSERVATION_COLLECTION → ASSERTION_EVALUATION → FAILURE_CLASSIFICATION → CERTIFICATION_RECORDING → REGRESSION_COMPARISON → REPORTING`. Academy FIRST FAILURE and the Phase 1–6 Brain FIRST FAILURE are separate and drillable.

The taxonomy distinguishes architectural, cognitive, evidence, context, memory, knowledge, planning, authority, policy, tool, verification, operational, measurement, experiment, market, economic uncertainty, QA, learning, management, harness, and inconclusive-evidence outcomes. Versioned baselines are inputs to comparison and are never overwritten by the runner. Reports expose verdict counts, earliest failure, ownership, evidence, drift, pending human review, Architecture Complete state, and Phase 8 permission.

## Gate, CTQs, and limitations

The Architecture Complete gate requires every machine-certifiable matrix row to PASS from Academy evidence. Human criteria remain authenticated-admin-owned. Until that acceptance is recorded, the maximum state is `CONDITIONAL_PASS`, the Phase 7 readiness gate is `CONDITIONAL_GO`, and `phase8Allowed` is false.

Deterministic CTQs are zero false machine PASS, evidence-free PASS, machine-approved human criteria, constitutional bypass, manufactured role reasoning, hidden-chain-of-thought dependencies, unclassified executed failures, fixture failures blamed on Brain, market/Brain misclassification, stale-as-current evidence, destructive contradiction removal, unknown-as-zero cost, projected-as-actual revenue, failed-verification success, Production self-QA PASS, Learning self-modification, Manager override, unauthorized memory exposure, cross-organization leakage, lost provenance, and FIRST FAILURE misclassification; all required deterministic Academy tests and canonical readiness validation must pass. These are conformance checks, not statistical capability claims.

Current fixtures, repositories, model adapters, and tools are synthetic/in-memory and single-process. No live provider quality, distributed durability, production deployment, browser/device experience, commercial demand, economic repeatability, or unrestricted autonomy is certified. Phase 8 is not implemented or authorized by this phase.
