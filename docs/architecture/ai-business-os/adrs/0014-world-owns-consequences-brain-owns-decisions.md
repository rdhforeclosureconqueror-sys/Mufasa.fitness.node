# ADR 0014: The simulated World owns consequences; the Brain owns decisions

## Status

Accepted for Phase 8 development. Human architectural acceptance remains separately pending.

## Context

Brain exams cannot establish that the complete organization remains coherent through offers, obligations, finite capacity, production, QA, delivery, payment, failure, and later replanning. A simulator that chooses the opportunity or grades its own result would only script an answer. A model that declares payment, delivery, or capacity would erase economic truth.

## Decision

Create a closed, offline `simulation` boundary. The World owns customer rules, hidden truth, simulated time, seeded chance, state transitions, capacity, obligations, deliveries, and the synthetic ledger. The Brain sees only public/discoverable observations and proposes decisions. The Phase 6 Organization authorizes and coordinates proposals as governed Work. The Phase 1 constitution controls permission. The Phase 7 Academy observes completed reports and judges declared invariants.

Every World mutation requires a versioned `SimulationAction` carrying actor, Work, authority, correlation, causation, and simulated time. World transitions retain before/after state and evidence. Projected, quoted, committed, incurred, pending, settled, and refunded economics remain distinct. QA requires an actor independent from production. Checkpoints record clock, event ordering, random state, ledger, obligations, capacity, and cycle.

## Consequences

Runs are deterministic for the same scenario, seed, ordering, configuration, and Brain actions. Hidden customer facts remain unavailable unless declared discoverable. Market rejection, technical failure, QA failure, cancellation, economic uncertainty, and harness failure stay distinct. Phase 8 can validate organism architecture without claiming profit, market demand, economic validation, or commercial readiness.

The World is intentionally not a strategy engine, the Academy is not an executor, and the Brain cannot rewrite facts. Phase 9, live tools, real people, real money, unrestricted autonomy, and irreversible effects remain prohibited pending a separate explicit authorization.
