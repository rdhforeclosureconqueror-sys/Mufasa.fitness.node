## Extraction rule

A Phase 1 item may move from **candidate** to direct **REUSE** only after its source implementation, dependencies, tests, license/ownership, failure behavior, and domain assumptions are recorded as evidence. Otherwise use an adapter, generalize behind a new contract, or reimplement the proven behavior with parity tests. No row authorizes moving domain code between repositories.
