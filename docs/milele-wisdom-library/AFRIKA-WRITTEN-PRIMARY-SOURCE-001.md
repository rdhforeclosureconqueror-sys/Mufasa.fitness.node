## Interpretation: proposed, not attributed as his direct words
- Candidate rule: evaluate a food by ingredient processing and substance, not only by whether it is sold as a health food.
- Candidate coaching principle: offer manageable, educational dietary changes rather than rigid immediate compliance.
- Neither rule should be deployed as a medical recommendation without independent review.

## Counterevidence and limits
- This interview contains first-person dietary testimony and opinions, not controlled evidence of disease outcomes.
- Raw-food and restrictive diets can carry nutritional and food-safety risks; historical advocacy does not establish universal suitability.
- The original recording of a talk by Afrika has **not** been listened to or timestamped. Do not report these written statements as lecture timestamps.
