# Milele Fit Wisdom Library — Public Source Research v0.1

## Purpose
Build Milele Fit's independent nutrition methodology by studying publicly accessible original teachings of Alfredo Bowman (Dr. Sebi) and Llaila O. Afrika. Neither educator endorses Milele Fit. Public accessibility does not imply permission to redistribute or train on full copyrighted works.

## Methodology map — provisional hypotheses, NOT attributed quotes
| Question | Sebi: candidate theme to investigate | Afrika: candidate theme to investigate | Evidence needed |

## Evidence protocol
For each claim record: source ID, URL, author/speaker, publication date if known, exact timestamp/page, short permitted quote or paraphrase, observation vs interpretation, corroborating/contradictory passages, and review status.
Statuses: DIRECT_SOURCE, RECONSTRUCTED_PRINCIPLE, MILELE_HYPOTHESIS, INDEPENDENTLY_EVALUATED_FINDING.
Never transform a pattern score into a probability of curing disease. Never silently upgrade hypotheses to findings. Preserve attribution internally and disclose sources when requested.

## Nutrition integration contract (future, not implemented here)
The nutrition coach may explain deterministic label facts and compare food with the evolving Milele standard. Nutrients and serving sizes must come from verified product data; uncertainty must be explicit. Research hypotheses cannot overwrite barcode facts or silently determine product approval. No medical cure claims or instructions to stop prescribed treatment.

## First acceptance milestone
1. Verify at least two freely viewable original recordings per educator.
2. Capture ten timestamped, source-grounded propositions per educator.
3. Compare agreement, disagreement and uncertainty in a food-selection matrix.
4. Human-review interpretations before public nutrition recommendations.
