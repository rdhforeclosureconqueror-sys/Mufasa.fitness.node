## Initial cross-source question grid
1. What observable traits are used to accept or reject a food?
2. What distinctions do speakers draw between whole, processed, and hybrid foods?
3. What role do minerals, preparation, and digestion play in their reasoning?
4. What counts as an observation, a personal experience, a mechanism, or a claimed outcome?
5. Do examples support the proposed classification rule consistently?
6. Where do speakers disagree or revise an earlier principle?

## Publication gate
Do not infer health efficacy from repetition, video popularity, or the number of educators who agree. Do not convert any untested botanical hypothesis into a cure or safety recommendation. Nutrition facts are taken from product labels, not these lectures. No implied endorsement. Link to originals rather than republishing copyrighted recordings.
