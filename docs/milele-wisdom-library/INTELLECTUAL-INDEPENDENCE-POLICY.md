# Milele Wisdom Library — Intellectual Independence Policy

**Historical accuracy is not conditional on endorsement by Western medicine or any other institution.**

1. **Preserve:** Record what Dr. Sebi and Dr. Llaila O. Afrika actually taught, with their own terminology, reasoning, classifications, observations, experiments and contradictions. Source verification establishes *who said what*, not whether a conventional institution agrees.
2. **Reconstruct:** Document each educator's own method and its internal logic before comparing it to other frameworks.
3. **Compare:** Examine agreements and disagreements between the educators without assigning automatic authority to either one.
4. **Investigate:** Record Milele's own hypotheses separately from historical teachings, and test them through transparent, reproducible observations where possible.
5. **Contextualize:** Contemporary scientific evidence, traditional knowledge, lived observation and open questions can be presented side by side with distinct provenance. Disagreement does not delete a teaching; absence of clinical confirmation is not proof of falsity.
6. **Protect:** A historical account may faithfully describe a cure claim or controversial theory while clearly attributing it. That does not authorize the AI to prescribe it as an established cure, replace medical care, or fabricate scanner nutrition facts.

## Two distinct gates
- **Attribution gate:** verified source content, exact locator and review; no mainstream-science approval requirement.
- **Application gate:** when moving from education to individualized health recommendations, separately review safety and evidence; product labels remain factual product data, not philosophical classifications.

The research library and the member-facing assistant must not confuse *accurate historical description* with *verified therapeutic efficacy*. Neither educator's intellectual contributions should be erased by this distinction.
