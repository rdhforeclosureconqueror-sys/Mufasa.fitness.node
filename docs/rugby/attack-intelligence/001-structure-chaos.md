## Original coaching text (verbatim)

**TWO DIFFERENT __PRACTICE__ MINDSETS: STRUCTURE & CHAOS**

**1.)    STRUCTURE Mindset**
When we practice **STRUCTURE**, the mindset is **APPLYING PRESSURE** through **STRUCTURE, PRECISION, and EXECUTION** according to our set-phase and phase-play structures.

We focus on our roles, positioning, timing, communication and decision-making. We build good habits through repetition until our structure becomes instinctive. BAM BAM!

**2.)    CHAOS Mindset**
When we practice **CHAOS**, the mindset shifts to **APPLYING PRESSURE AT PACE**, using **HIGH LEVELS OF SKILL, ADAPTABILITY, CREATIVITY & PROBLEM-SOLVING.**  We use STRUCTURE to achieve CHAOS – and we want to get there as rapidly as possible.

Then we embrace uncertainty, react to changing pictures, make decisions under pressure and find solutions. Mistakes are part of learning. Be brave, experiment and back yourself!  BAM BAM!

**SUMMARY:**
**BOTH ARE EQUALLY IMPORTANT.**

***Structure gives us the foundation to play with confidence. Chaos teaches us to adapt when the game doesn't follow the script.***

**__THE CHALLENGE__**: Know what we're practicing, bring the right mindset, and commit fully to it.

**STRUCTURE PROTECTS THE PLAYER. CHAOS DEVELOPS THE PLAYER.**

## Source-grounded learning objectives

- Distinguish applying pressure through structure/precision/execution from applying pressure at pace through skill/adaptability/creativity/problem-solving.
- Identify roles, positioning, timing, communication and decision-making as structure practice emphases.
- Explain why both practice mindsets are equally important.
- Recognize that uncertainty and mistakes can be part of chaos practice.
