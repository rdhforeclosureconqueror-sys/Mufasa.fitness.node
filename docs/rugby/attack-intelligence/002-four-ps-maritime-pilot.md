## Original coaching text (verbatim)

Our attack works when we all understand our responsibilities. 
1. PILOTS steer the structure
    PILOTS don’t chase highlights, focus on winning the next 5 minutes
2. PLAYMAKERS play the picture
3. POWER makes the defense retreat
4. PACE makes the defense late

1. PILOT (GAME DRIVERS)
DRIVE YOUR AREA: OWN THE GAME PLAN & TACTICS (must have a valid reason to go off script)
•  9/10/15: Steer territory, structure, tempo, and connections between phases.
•  Lineout Caller: Own the lineout - calls, timing, and execution.
•  Hooker/Props: Own scrum setup, timing, and execution.
•  Centers: Organize your pod’s shape, timing, and attacking options.
•  All Pilots: Read, communicate, adapt, and solve problems. Next man up when roles change.

2. PLAYMAKER (RUGBY IQ)
EVERY PLAYER IS A PLAYMAKER
•  Read the picture: Scan early and recognize opportunities.
•  Win the decision: Choose the best option, with or without the ball.
•  Manipulate defenders: Threaten, hold, fix, link, or create space.
•  Think ahead: Win this moment while setting up the next.

3. POWER (BRUTAL PHYSICALITY)
EVERY PLAYER MUST BRING “POWER”
•  Win the collision: Strong body position, leg drive, and aggressive intent.
•  Win the gain line: Go forward and put the defense on the back foot.
•  Win the breakdown: Arrive with purpose; secure fast, clean ball and protect momentum.
•  Make them retreat: Drive defenders back, compress their shape, and create space.
•  Reload and repeat: Get back into the fight and win the next collision.

4. PACE (SPEED KILLS)
EVERY PLAYER CREATES PACE
•  Think fast: Scan and decide before the defense adjusts.
•  Move bodies fast: Accelerate, reload, realign, and support with urgency.
•  Move the ball fast: Pass with purpose before defenders reset.
•  Win ruck speed: Arrive early, secure clean ball, and recycle quickly.
•  Make them late: Keep defenders turning, folding, and chasing.**THE MARITIME PILOT**

A maritime PILOT is an expert navigator who follows established **structure**, sets direction, and guides a vessel through challenging waters.

They read conditions, anticipate risks, and apply their knowledge, plans, and procedures to ***make high-reward, low-risk decisions***.

***They trust the structure, adapt with purpose, and keep the vessel on course***.

**THE RUGBY PILOT (GAME DRIVER)**

A rugby PILOT is a game driver who **follows our attacking structure**, sets direction, and guides the team through changing game conditions.

They read the defensive picture, anticipate pressure, and apply our game plan, tactics, shapes, and options to*** make high-reward, low-risk decisions***.

***They trust the structure, adapt with purpose, and keep the team on course***.

**STRUCTURE PROTECTS THE PLAYER.**

## Source-grounded takeaways

- Pilots are designated game drivers (9/10/15, lineout caller, hooker/props, centers, and role replacements).
- Every player is a playmaker and must contribute power and pace.
- Pilot decisions must follow the game plan, with a valid reason for deviation.
- Pilot analogy emphasizes navigation, anticipating risk, and high-reward low-risk decisions.
- This source defines responsibilities, not full running lines or detailed named plays.
