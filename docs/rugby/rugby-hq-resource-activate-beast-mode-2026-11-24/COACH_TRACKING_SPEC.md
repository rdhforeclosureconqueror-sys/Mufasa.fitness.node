# Coach Tracking Specification

## Quality scoring scale

1 = poor / unsafe  
2 = inconsistent / needs regression  
3 = acceptable but not ready to progress  
4 = good / progression-ready standard  
5 = excellent / advanced standard

## Scores to track

- control
- balance
- technique
- trunk stability
- lower-limb alignment
- landing quality
- change-of-direction quality
- neck control
- fatigue breakdown

## Readiness and safety

- soreness level
- fatigue level
- pain flag
- injury concern
- concussion concern
- coach notes

## Progression statuses

- Not assessed
- Learning
- Needs regression
- Hold current level
- Ready with reduced volume
- Ready to progress
- Advanced
- Beast Mode candidate

## Dashboard needs

Team view:
- current team level
- percentage ready to progress
- athletes held back
- pain/injury flags
- average quality scores
- attendance rate
- weekly usage count

Athlete view:
- current stage
- current level
- weeks at level
- score trend
- attendance trend
- recommendation
- coach notes
- safety flags
