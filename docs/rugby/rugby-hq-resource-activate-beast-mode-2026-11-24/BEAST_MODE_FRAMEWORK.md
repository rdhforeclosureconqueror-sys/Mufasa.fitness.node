# Beast Mode Framework

## Definition

Beast Mode is repeated controlled violence with clean mechanics.

It is not simply harder workouts. It means the athlete can repeatedly express high rugby power under fatigue, chaos, instability and contact-like demands while preserving posture, balance, lower-limb alignment, trunk control and decision-making.

## Ladder

1. Functional Stability
2. Maximum Stability
3. Strength Stabilization
4. Maximum Strength
5. Power
6. Maximum Power
7. Beast Mode

## Stage meanings

Functional Stability: own basic position.
Maximum Stability: stay organized under stability challenge.
Strength Stabilization: produce force without losing alignment.
Maximum Strength: produce high force without losing structure.
Power: express force quickly.
Maximum Power: repeat explosive outputs with quality.
Beast Mode: repeat high-power rugby actions under fatigue, chaos and contact-like demands.

## Non-negotiable rule

No athlete enters Beast Mode if they cannot preserve Activate movement-quality standards.
