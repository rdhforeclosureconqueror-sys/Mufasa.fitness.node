# Rugby HQ Resources 5 — Club Standards & Compliance Matrix

Status: operating reference  
Audience: Rugby HQ bot, club administrators, coaches, product/marketing agents.

## Purpose

The core question from the thread was not merely "which certifications must a coach take?" It was:

> What is the club actually expected or required to have in place after the education is complete?

This document keeps that distinction explicit.

## 1. Required label system

Every Rugby HQ rule/process card should use one of these labels:

- **REQUIRED — GOVERNING BODY / CLUB COMPLIANCE**
- **REQUIRED — LAW / COMPETITION**
- **RECOMMENDED — GOVERNING BODY BEST PRACTICE**
- **POCKET PT INTERNAL STANDARD**
- **CURRENT-RULE-VERIFY**

Never promote an internal Pocket PT badge as an official credential unless an external authority formally recognizes it.

## 2. Safeguarding / SafeSport

THREAD-DERIVED POSITION:
Safeguarding obligations and reporting responsibilities are real club-level responsibilities for covered participants/activities, but the club is not necessarily required to use a specific software product or a specific title such as "Safeguarding Officer" in every context.

Rugby HQ value:
- publish/acknowledge code of conduct;
- make reporting pathway visible;
- record poor-practice concerns;
- route suspected abuse appropriately;
- preserve confidential action log;
- restrict case access;
- track required training;
- maintain risk assessments.

Safe sales language:
> Rugby HQ helps clubs operationalize and document safeguarding responsibilities.

Do not say:
> USA Rugby requires every club to use this exact Rugby HQ safeguarding workflow.

CURRENT-RULE-VERIFY:
Check current USA Rugby SafeSport policy, U.S. Center for SafeSport obligations, state law, youth rules and club/competition requirements before enforcement.

## 3. Rugby Ready

THREAD-DERIVED POSITION:
Rugby Ready is part of coach safety education, but the thread did not establish a universal rule requiring every player to hold a Rugby Ready certificate or every club to maintain a formal player-by-player Rugby Ready database.

Rugby HQ use:
Translate Rugby Ready education into:
- player profile;
- environment safety;
- movement readiness;
- tackle/contact progressions;
- scrum competency;
- emergency preparation;
- incident/welfare workflows.

Safe language:
> Rugby HQ gives coaches a structured way to apply and document Rugby Ready principles across the squad.

Do not say:
> Every player must possess an official Rugby Ready certificate.

## 4. Front-row / Scrum suitability

THREAD-DERIVED POSITION:
This is stronger than a generic best-practice recommendation. The team has a playing-law responsibility to field appropriately trained/experienced front-row players for contested scrums.

Rugby HQ use:
- internal Scrum Ready stages;
- video/evidence;
- coach verification;
- position-specific history;
- current/expired status;
- role eligibility gate.

Safe language:
> Rugby HQ provides structured evidence to support the team's responsibility to determine whether a front-row player is suitably trained and experienced.

Do not say:
> Pocket PT Scrum Ready is an official USA Rugby certification.

CURRENT-RULE-VERIFY:
Always check current World Rugby Laws, USA Rugby variations, age-grade rules and competition regulations.

## 5. Concussion process

THREAD-DERIVED POSITION:
Recognise-and-Remove and return-to-play obligations are safety requirements; the exact protocol/timeline varies and changes.

Rugby HQ use:
- incident capture;
- immediate removal;
- red-flag routing;
- follow-up;
- protocol version;
- staged return;
- medical document record;
- no coach override of active restriction.

Safe language:
> Rugby HQ supports concussion recognition, removal, documentation and protocol tracking; it does not diagnose or medically clear.

CURRENT-RULE-VERIFY:
Version USA Rugby / World Rugby / local law / youth protocol.

## 6. Activate

THREAD-DERIVED POSITION:
Activate is a governing-body injury-prevention / preparation program and strong best practice. The thread did not establish it as a universal USA Rugby club requirement.

Rugby HQ label:
**RECOMMENDED — GOVERNING BODY BEST PRACTICE**

System:
- Activate-Aligned Adult Foundation;
- coach cue checklist;
- movement-quality tracking;
- progress/hold/regress;
- match-day/training delivery.

Do not label:
**MANDATORY USA RUGBY ACTIVATE COMPLIANCE** unless current official rules explicitly say so.

## 7. Tackle Ready

THREAD-DERIVED POSITION:
Use as a best-practice technical framework, not an assumed mandatory player credential.

Rugby HQ:
- tracking;
- preparation;
- connection;
- acceleration;
- finish;
- evidence progression;
- safe tackle-in-space / contact pathways.

Label:
**RECOMMENDED — GOVERNING BODY BEST PRACTICE**
plus
**POCKET PT INTERNAL COMPETENCY** for the scoring/progression engine.

## 8. Backs Rugby Ready

This is a POCKET PT INTERNAL STANDARD.

Purpose:
Correct forward-heavy readiness models by giving backs an equally rigorous pathway for:
- defensive IQ;
- tackling in space;
- attack IQ;
- ball skills;
- movement/evasion;
- role-specific skill;
- off-ball work;
- position overlays.

Never market this as an official World Rugby/USA Rugby credential.

## 9. Beast Mode

This is a POCKET PT INTERNAL PERFORMANCE SYSTEM.

Keep clearly separate from Activate.

Label:
**POCKET PT INTERNAL STANDARD**

## 10. Coach credential tracker

Rugby HQ should distinguish:
- mandatory/current external credentials;
- recommended education;
- internal club development.

CURRENT-RULE-VERIFY:
Before blocking a coach, recheck current USA Rugby requirements and renewal periods.

## 11. Club sales positioning

Preferred:
> Rugby HQ does not replace Rugby Xplorer, USA Rugby or World Rugby. It gives the club an operating layer for translating coaching education, player-welfare responsibilities, competency evidence and team decisions into repeatable processes.

Preferred:
> The governing body teaches the standard. Rugby HQ helps the club implement, evidence and manage it.

Avoid:
> USA Rugby mandates Rugby HQ.

Avoid:
> Pocket PT certifies official front-row eligibility.

## 12. Bot behavior

If the bot is asked "is this mandatory?":
1. Identify jurisdiction / competition / age group if relevant.
2. Check current governing source before answering.
3. Return the exact label:
   - required;
   - recommended;
   - internal.
4. State whether Rugby HQ is the required mechanism or merely a tool for operationalizing the requirement.
