## Review queue
- barbell_deadlift: conventional bilateral floor pull; revise provisional quadriceps role, evaluate gluteus maximus, hamstrings and erector spinae separately (E13,E14). EMG does not determine agonist versus stabilizer.
- romanian_deadlift: bilateral hip hinge, slight knee flexion; hamstrings and gluteus maximus hip extension candidates, erector spinae trunk support (E13-E15).
- dumbbell_rdl: bilateral dumbbells, hip hinge; same hypotheses but indirect evidence from barbell studies, verify implement and load placement.
- glute_bridge: bilateral supine bridge, knee angle specified; gluteus maximus and hamstrings candidates; multifidus stabilization review (E16-E18). Do not extrapolate hip thrust or single-leg bridge directly.
- barbell_lunge: define forward stepping with barbell; front and rear limb roles separately, quadriceps and gluteus maximus candidates (E16,E19); gluteus medius stabilization hypothesis requires direct evidence. Bilateral display must not imply equal activation.
