# Pressing evidence package 001 — research draft, not approval

Sources reviewed:
- E1 https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6728153/ — 20 trained men, comparison across loads; pectoralis major, anterior deltoid and triceps involvement. Limited population and measured muscles.
- E2 https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25419894/ — 29 fit men, pressing variants and trunk EMG; stability and resistance alter recruitment. Not direct evidence for every incline configuration.
- E3 https://doi.org/10.1080/02640414.2010.543916 — 12 trained men, dumbbell/barbell/Smith comparisons; biceps activity increased with stability demand and triceps activity was lower with dumbbells. Smith is not equivalent to a seated chest press.
- E4 https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35599715/ — scoping review of push-up variations; serratus anterior prominent in incline and push-up plus. Heterogeneous protocols.
- E5 https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29809073/ — 12 active men, hand position and core activation influence comparisons.

## Exercise-specific review queue
- push_up: standard floor, bilateral hands, rigid trunk. Pectoralis major shoulder horizontal adduction, triceps elbow extension, anterior deltoid assists shoulder motion (E1,E5). Rectus abdominis anti-extension candidate (E2,E5). Triceps may be co-primary depending on role definition. Serratus anterior needed in region backlog (E2,E4).
- incline_push_up: hands elevated on stable support; record height. Pectoralis, triceps, anterior deltoid candidates extrapolated from floor pressing (E1,E4); trunk role and serratus demand require variant-specific review (E4). Do not assume floor equivalence.
- bench_press: flat barbell, bilateral grip; chest, triceps and anterior deltoid candidates (E1,E3); stabilizer roles unresolved. Triceps co-primary question remains.
- dumbbell_bench_press: flat bench, bilateral independent dumbbells; chest, anterior deltoid and triceps candidates (E3); biceps stabilization hypothesis, not an established role from EMG alone.
- machine_chest_press: seated guided press, machine geometry unspecified. Chest, triceps and anterior deltoid hypotheses only; E3 Smith comparison is indirect and insufficient for this machine. Obtain direct evidence and specific machine technique.

No role hierarchy or geometry approved. EMG is not muscle force or proof of role. Missing serratus region must not be substituted with another muscle. No production mappings enabled. Qualified review remains required.
