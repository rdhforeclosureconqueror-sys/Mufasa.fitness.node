# Pulling evidence package 002 — research draft
Sources inspected (abstracts):
- E6 Fenwick et al. 2009 https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19197209/ : seven men; bent-over row produced broad back activation and greatest lumbar load among three tested rows. Small sample; no elastic band condition.
- E7 McGill et al. 2014 https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jelekin.2014.06.002 : 14 men; pulling tasks, technique and support affect muscular demands and spinal loading. Not a direct elastic-band comparison.
- E8 Handa et al. 2005 https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jspfsm/54/2/54_2_159/_pdf/-char/ja : eight trained men, five pulling exercises at 70% 1RM, biceps, latissimus and trapezius EMG. Limited measured muscles; front pulldown not proof of wide-grip-specific effects.
