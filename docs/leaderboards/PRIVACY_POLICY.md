# Universal leaderboard privacy policy

Participation is opt-out/private-mode capable. The default alias is `Member ••••`; a member may choose a safe profile display name, but email-shaped names are never emitted. Administrative, banned, opted-out, and zero-XP accounts are excluded. Responses reveal no email, location, body, nutrition, assessment, or health data. The authenticated requester receives only a public standings view plus their own position.
