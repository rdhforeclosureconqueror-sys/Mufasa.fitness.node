# SMART_SCOUT Platinum architecture upgrade

## Scope and preserved architecture

SMART_SCOUT remains the `DISCOVER_CANDIDATE` role configuration on the single shared Business OS runtime. Work and artifacts continue through the organizational coordinator, constitutional kernel, canonical execution registry, evidence/provenance, audit, and Academy seams. This upgrade adds no service, Brain, database, policy engine, direct provider call, Scout-to-Analyst invocation, or private truth store. Phase 9B-A/Test A, its seven-day trial, 30-day challenge, Stripe checkout, and Test B state are unchanged.

## Contracts and policy

`ScoutSourceObservation`, `OpportunityCandidate`, `ScoutOutcomeFeedback`, and `ScoutSourceHealth` are versioned canonical records. The policy ranks verified outcomes above behavioral and attention evidence, but preserves contradictions. Attention is not intent; intent is not purchase. Controlled validation and synthetic outcomes cannot become independent market evidence. Assessments record every factor, basis, weight, version, limitation, prior-assessment link, and change summary. Earlier observations are append-only and never rewritten.

SMART_SCOUT may observe, normalize, classify, compare, identify candidates, state uncertainty, request evidence, and recommend an Analyst question. Its terminal handoff is a governed candidate artifact. It may not select or launch campaigns, contact people, publish, spend, price, activate products, modify readiness, declare profit, override Analyst dissent, apply Learning proposals, or browse without an approved source.

Missing authorization can never be `OPERATIONAL`. Provider acceptance and tool success are not business verification or market success. An outage is not no demand; rate limiting is not rejection; missing data is not zero.

## Read-only capability boundary

The execution registry defines ten narrow capability contracts and nine tool contracts. They are registered as unavailable and design-stage by default; each becomes selectable only when its implementation, configuration, and verification evidence are explicitly supplied. Their boundaries remain `OBSERVE` or `RECOMMEND`, zero acquisition spend, low risk, constitutional authority, explicit policy, verification, evidence, audit, diagnostics, and kill-switch applicability.

## Diagnostics and certification truth

Scout diagnostics are ordered:

`MISSION → AUTHORITY → SOURCE_REGISTERED → SOURCE_AUTHORIZED → SOURCE_READ → SOURCE_VERIFIED → NORMALIZED → FRESHNESS → PRIVACY → DEDUPLICATION → PRODUCT_FIT → READINESS → ASSESSMENT → ARTIFACT_VALIDATED → HANDOFF → FEEDBACK_AVAILABLE → REPORTING`

The earliest causal failure is retained and downstream checks become `BLOCKED`; `NOT_APPLICABLE` remains distinct. The initial truthful FIRST FAILURE for Search Console and GA4 is `SOURCE_AUTHORIZED / HUMAN_CONFIGURATION_REQUIRED`.

The 24 deterministic/adversarial records are canonical Academy scenario specifications. They do not certify behavior until the canonical Academy runner executes every registered executor, evaluates every assertion, and records evidence. The independent gates are `SCOUT_PLATINUM_ARCHITECTURE_READY`, `SCOUT_APPROVED_SOURCE_CONFIGURED`, `SCOUT_LIVE_SOURCE_VERIFIED`, `SCOUT_OUTCOME_FEEDBACK_VERIFIED`, and `SCOUT_PLATINUM_CERTIFIED`. At this commit architecture certification, live-source verification, closed-loop feedback, authenticated human acceptance, and final Platinum certification remain blocked. Definitions, mocks, booleans, and evidence-reference strings cannot satisfy these gates. Mocks, configuration alone, synthetic evidence, and controlled Test A cannot satisfy those gates.

## Coordinator precondition audit

All four identified prerequisites required repairs: assignment now resolves an active grant for the correct actor, action, and organization; execution is reserved before awaiting the runtime; every artifact is validated and staged before any publication; and QA artifacts/reviews require the canonical verdict enum. Regression tests cover each repair.

## Human and operational actions remaining

1. An authorized administrator must configure official read-only Search Console and/or GA4 credentials and property access.
2. A real approved-source read must produce independently verified provider evidence.
3. A later independent experiment outcome must return through the privacy-limited Learning/Economics contract.
4. An authenticated authorized administrator must record required human acceptance. Machine tooling cannot self-approve it.
