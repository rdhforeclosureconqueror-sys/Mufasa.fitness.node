# Known Limitations and Version 1 Exclusions

* Cross-browser authenticated acceptance and screenshots are incomplete.
* Manual accessibility and production operational sign-offs are incomplete.
* Profile & Settings has no dedicated member page.
* Gamification flags default closed and require coordinated activation.
* Camera-enabled Yoga UI is not integrated; camera-optional completion is available and professionally gated rules remain inactive.
* Production latency, database migrations/backups, AI provider configuration/costs, and monitoring require environment validation.
* Nutrition records do not award XP; Nutrition Engine/rewards are excluded.
* Running/trails, wearables, social, clinical/corrective assessment, gymnastics, advanced unreviewed Yoga, and unlicensed legacy media are excluded.
