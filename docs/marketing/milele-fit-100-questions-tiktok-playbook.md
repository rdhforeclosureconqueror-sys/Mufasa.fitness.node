## Purpose

Celebrate partial consistency and encourage people to return without shame. Avoid implying every feature is live or included in a trial until production acceptance confirms it.

## Creative rules
- 9:16 portrait; one relatable human moment, preferably diverse Black men/women with natural, welcoming expressions.
- Main image: one hook, one short takeaway, one CTA; target 15–30 words. Put explanations in the caption.
- Green/gold Milele Fit identity; readable type; don't clutter images with a feature catalog.
- Caption: question, practical 2–3 step answer, empathetic encouragement, one relevant route, 4–8 topical hashtags.
- Do not promise treatment, cure, instant results, or a fully accessible seven-day premium trial without verification.

## Complete launch post — Question 1
**Question:** Where do I start if I've never worked out?

**Hook:** “Ever walked into a gym, looked at all those machines, and thought… I don't even know what I'm doing here? 😂”

**Answer:** Start with ten minutes of comfortable movement such as walking. Learn beginner movements like chair squats and wall push-ups, using modifications that feel safe. Try two or three manageable sessions a week and increase gradually. Consistency matters more than exhaustion.

**Emotional message:** You don't have to be in shape to belong. If you managed a week and fell off, that week still counts; practice returning.

**Image concept:** 9:16 photo of a relatable Black beginner looking uncertain but welcomed in a gym. Headline: “NEW TO FITNESS? YOU BELONG HERE TOO.” Subline: “Start small. Build confidence. Grow together.” Green/gold branding, large type, minimal copy.
