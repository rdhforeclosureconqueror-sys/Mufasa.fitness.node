# Pocket PT Rugby Coaching OS — V4 Backline Balance Upgrade

V4 closes the forwards/backs development imbalance by adding **Backs Rugby Ready** as a first-class pathway.

## Added

- Universal Backline Skill/IQ Rating (1–99) with evidence-based weighted categories.
- Defensive IQ, Tackling in Space, Attack IQ, Ball Skills, Movement & Evasion, Role-Specific Skill.
- Hard starter-eligibility gate when Tackling in Space is below 70.
- Position overlays for 10, 12, 13, wing and 15.
- Explicit offense-without-the-ball assessment.
- Explicit offense-with-the-ball assessment.
- Separate Backline Athletic Index with backs-specific weighting.
- Drill / scrimmage / match / video evidence model.
- Player-facing next actions.
- Coach Guide documentation and production data-entry specification.
- Standalone interactive Backline Ready prototype at `standalone/backs-ready.html`.

## Architecture rule

Forwards and backs now share the same philosophy but not identical role demands:

- Forwards: scrum/contact/unit readiness pathways.
- Backs: space/decision/tackle-in-space/role-IQ readiness pathways.
- All players: movement, conditioning, tackle fundamentals, welfare, safeguarding and evidence history.

Skill/IQ and athleticism remain separate. Safety restrictions and hard gates cannot be averaged away by performance scores.
