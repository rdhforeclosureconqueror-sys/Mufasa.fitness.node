Coach session view

Four-part delivery structure

Part A

Running preparation

Small-sided games, movement prep, snake runs and general running activity.

Part B

Mobility · stability · balance

Mobility, single-leg control and posture-focused work using Activate 8 cues.

Part C

Resistance · power · dynamic balance

Partner resistance, Nordic work, hops, jumps, mirroring and explosive preparation.

Part D

Neck · sprint · high output

Neck control, repeated sprint work, bounding and higher-output preparation where appropriate.

Default engine logic

Progression is earned — not time served

PROGRESS when: attendance_rate ≥ 75% AND control ≥ 4 AND balance ≥ 4 AND technique ≥ 4 AND no active pain / injury flag AND coach_approved = true TEAM: if ≥ 70% of squad is ready → progress team while keeping athletes who need it on scaled versions. REGRESS / REDUCE: if movement quality < 3 OR pain flag active OR athlete cannot maintain required Activate cues → reduce reps/duration, regress exercise or return to prior level.
Pocket PT extension — not World Rugby Activate

Beast Mode Rugby Performance System

Beast Mode begins only after the athlete can preserve Activate-quality movement. Harder is not the goal. The goal is higher force, speed and rugby complexity without losing structure.

1 · Functional StabilityOwn clean foundational positions.
2 · Maximum StabilityMaintain structure under perturbation, instability, fatigue or chaos.
3 · Strength StabilizationProduce force while maintaining alignment and control.
4 · Maximum StrengthProduce higher force without losing structure.
5 · PowerProduce force rapidly with quality.
6 · Maximum PowerRepeat explosive force while preserving mechanics.
7 · Beast ModeRepeat high-power rugby actions under fatigue, chaos, contact and decisions.
