# Eight Pillars — Lesson-first story production standard

## Source of truth and scope
The **lesson**, not the story, is the reusable curriculum unit. Each of eight weeks has one major Adinkra anchor, seven supporting daily symbol lessons, and a complementary weekly yoga limb. The original Akan meanings must not be changed to fit a story. The pairing with the Eight Limbs is Milele Fit's contemporary educational bridge, not an asserted historical equivalence.

**Status:** Editorial specification. The eight reconstructed story drafts currently in the repository are **not approved full manuscripts**. The existing live PAC Eight Limbs page and the newer African Wisdom pillar index use different week-to-symbol mappings. Do not silently merge these tracks or overwrite the original Eight Limbs curriculum. Verify the approved Adinkra PDF and weekly mapping before publication.

## Daily lesson contract (56 lessons)
Each day must have:
1. Week number, day number (Sunday through Saturday), weekly anchor and daily supporting symbol.
2. Symbol's verified traditional name, meaning, and approved artwork/reference.
3. Principle: one clear takeaway consistent with that meaning.
4. Child-friendly explanation and adult explanation.
5. Concrete example of a decision, conflict, or behavior.
6. Practical application or guided practice, with an appropriate yoga/breath/meditation connection.
7. Affirmation.
8. Awareness challenge or action to try that day.
9. Reflection question and optional journal prompt.
10. Link to the weekly story's corresponding scene or turning point.

## Weekly story contract
- 8 stories total; one per week.
- Minimum **10 minutes**, maximum **15 minutes** when actually narrated; aim for approximately **12 minutes**.
- Draft for roughly 1,400–1,600 words, then measure a full read aloud with dialogue and intentional pauses. Word count alone does not certify duration.
- Begin with an emotionally compelling hook and an understandable central problem.
- Build seven meaningful scenes, choices, challenges, or discoveries illustrating the seven daily lessons. The supporting symbols do not have to be announced by name inside the fictional narrative; a separate scene-to-symbol guide must identify each.
- Give the weekly anchor symbol a unifying role in the character's transformation.
- Preserve character agency, realistic setbacks, consequences, community relationships, and a satisfying but non-magical resolution.
- The story may use any era or setting, and characters can vary. The lesson, cultural accuracy, and seven-scene mapping must remain stable across alternate stories.
- Close with a clear moral in natural language, a practical invitation to act, a reflection question, and an affirmation.
- Keep story text and teacher notes separate so the story reads naturally aloud.
- Maintain editable written transcript and optional narration. A device speech voice is **not** a permanent cached recording; approved audio requires persistent storage.

## Teacher facilitation contract
**Hook → explain → discuss → embody → reflect → take-home action.**
Weekly class: introduce anchor symbol and its verified meaning, distinguish the yoga limb's tradition, read/listen to the weekly story, discuss the seven supporting lessons, embody through appropriate movement/breath/stillness, invite reflection, and assign a small action. Do not force all 56 lessons into one class.

## Story review checklist
- [ ] Weekly anchor meaning verified against the approved reference.
- [ ] All seven supporting symbols verified and mapped to seven story moments.
- [ ] Seven daily lesson contracts complete.
- [ ] No fabricated cultural history or invented symbol meanings.
- [ ] Story has a hook, stakes, change, and earned resolution.
- [ ] 10–15 minute narration confirmed by timed read.
- [ ] Child and adult explanations work independently of story.
- [ ] Affirmations, awareness challenges, reflections and practices align.
- [ ] Story transcript reviewed, approved, and versioned.
- [ ] Audio asset, if published, is approved, accessible and cached.

## Editorial sequence
First verify the complete 56-day symbol mapping against the approved PDF and distinguish the two program tracks. Then complete Week 1's seven daily lesson contracts and revise its story to the 10–15 minute standard. Repeat week by week. Only after editorial approval publish narration or enable member-facing release.
