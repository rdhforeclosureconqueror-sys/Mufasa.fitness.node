# Eight Pillars of African Wisdom — curriculum outline

**Status:** Foundation outline for review. This is an independent African-centered educational program, not a course structured around yoga's Eight Limbs. Adinkra meanings and artwork require checking against the approved reference PDF before final publication.

## Program outcomes
Across eight weeks, learners should understand the weekly Adinkra symbols, recognize the values represented by seven supporting daily symbols, apply the lessons to everyday decisions, practice self-reflection, and demonstrate growth through observable actions. Stories are teaching vehicles, not the source of the lesson.

## Weekly anchors
| Week | Major Adinkra | Core educational theme |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ani Bere A Enso Gya | Character and self-mastery |
| 2 | Nkonsonkonson | Unity and community |
| 3 | Sankofa | Wisdom from the past |
| 4 | Aya | Resilience and perseverance |
| 5 | Akoma | Love, care, and compassion |
| 6 | Akoben | Courage and responsibility |
| 7 | Abe Dua | Resourcefulness and collective prosperity |
| 8 | Gye Nyame | Faith and spiritual understanding |

## Required outline for each week
1. **Weekly anchor:** name, verified traditional meaning, approved artwork, why it matters, and the central question.
2. **Learning goals:** what a learner should be able to explain, recognize, and do by the end of the week.
3. **Seven supporting symbols (Sunday–Saturday):** each symbol's verified name and meaning, specific lesson, and how it supports the weekly anchor.
4. **Seven daily lesson cards:** age-appropriate explanation for child and adult, relatable scenario, practical application, affirmation, awareness challenge, reflection, and observable evidence of learning.
5. **Weekly story outline:** an engaging central problem and seven consequential story beats, one per daily lesson, with the anchor as the overarching transformation. Allow multiple alternative stories to teach the same lessons.
6. **Narration:** one complete story of 10–15 minutes when timed aloud; aim for 12 minutes, often roughly 1,400–1,600 words depending on pace. Readable transcript and voice playback.
7. **Teacher guide:** discussion prompts, how to connect each story beat to the actual symbol meaning, activities, misconceptions to avoid, and take-home application.
8. **Review gate:** cultural verification, seven-of-seven lesson coverage, measured narration duration, editorial approval, and recorded version.

## Build order
1. Verify the existing approved 56-day symbol sequence and original meanings.
2. Write the eight weekly lesson outlines, without rewriting stories yet.
3. Expand each week's seven daily lesson cards.
4. Revise its story to demonstrate the approved lessons in 10–15 minutes.
5. Review Week 1 together, then repeat week by week. Approved audio comes after story signoff.

**Separation:** The older PAC Eight Limbs + Adinkra wellness course remains a separate program. Do not automatically substitute its symbols or yoga-limb progression into Eight Pillars.
