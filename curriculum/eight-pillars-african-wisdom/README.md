# Milele Fit — Eight Pillars of African Wisdom

An original eight-week, 56-day wellness education journey grounded in Akan Adinkra teachings.

## Editorial principles

- Adinkra symbols and their traditional meanings lead the curriculum; yoga, meditation, pranayama and sound-bowl practices may accompany the lessons but do not redefine them.
- The eight-pillar grouping and stories are original Milele Fit educational framing, **not** a claim about an established Akan eight-pillar tradition.
- Do not alter a traditional symbol meaning to fit a story. In particular, **Sankofa** is returning to retrieve valuable knowledge from the past for the future.
- Verify symbol artwork and meanings against the approved source PDF before publishing.
- Target approximately 10 minutes per story read aloud, never more than 15 minutes. Include seven daily teaching connections, a five-minute reflection, and a closing affirmation.
- Preserve approved story manuscripts verbatim in separate files; revisions should be reviewable.
